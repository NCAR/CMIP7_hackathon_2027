About

The goal of this hackathon is to develop project ideas and collaborations towards analysis of the CMIP7 simulations now being produced, particularly the representation of historical trends and variability in this new generation of models. It will include some lectures and demonstrations on how to use and analyze CMIP data, but the bulk of the time will be spent on collaborative projects.

Collaborate

Join a small team around a science question and work on it together over the event.

Learn

Get hands-on experience accessing and analyzing CMIP7 data with modern tools.

Share

Present results at the end of the event and publish your team's code openly.

Apply

Participation is by application, and applications are due {{ e.application_deadline }}. The hackathon is open to all career stages.

Capacity at each hub is limited, and a limited amount of travel support is available thanks to Spark Climate Solutions and the CMIP IPO. Priority for travel support will be given to early-career researchers (within 10 years of PhD).

If we receive more applications than places, or more requests for travel support than funding available, we will select participants based on career stage and the relevance of their interests and proposed research to the hackathon goals.
